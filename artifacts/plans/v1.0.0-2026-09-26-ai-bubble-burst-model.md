## Risk & Mitigations

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Model Sensitivity to Arbitrary Parameters** | Output could look speculative rather than grounded | Anchor all baseline parameters in empirical data (BLS for wages, 10-K/10-Q SEC filings for Big-4 CapEx, CoreWeave debt disclosures, and FactSet S&P 500 multiples). |
| **KaTeX Markdown Rendering Collisions** | Currency unescaped `$` destroys table formatting | Strictly escape every dollar sign `\$` in markdown artifacts and UI tables per workspace rules. |
| **Over-simplifying Bank Exposure** | Conflating direct loans with fund leverage | Clearly partition the debt stack into direct loans, warehouse lines, and indirect fund subscription facilities. |
